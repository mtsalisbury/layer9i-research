Open research initiative

Atlas Research.Published openly, challenged openly.

This is the research arm of Atlas Identity: papers, articles, diagrams, and open technical review of whether the Internet is missing a native concept of Personal Internet Presence—one that belongs to an individual rather than a network, application, employer, government, or service provider. For what is actually built and running today, see the Atlas Identity company site.

Published openly for criticism from networking, security, cloud, identity, privacy, distributed-systems, and Internet-architecture professionals.

Research principles

Use what exists. Challenge what is missing.

01

Person-centred

The individual is the persistent point of reference—not the provider.

02

Interoperable

Atlas should leverage existing identity, network, security, and cloud systems.

03

Privacy-preserving

Recognition must not require universal disclosure, tracking, or centralized control.

04

Open to failure

The research must expose assumptions and accept that parts of the thesis may be wrong.

Open technical review

The objective is not to prove Atlas correct.

The objective is to discover where it is wrong.

Useful criticism includes

  • What already exists?
  • Which assumptions are incorrect?
  • Where would this architecture fail?
  • What privacy, governance, or abuse risks are missing?
  • Which standards should Atlas build upon?
